Mission Statement

KinCare connects families with vetted, body-cam verified caregivers for babysitting, pet care, transportation, senior companionship, home help, and tutoring — making quality care accessible, transparent, and affordable for every family.

How the System Operates — The Uber/DoorDash Model

Family (User App)

  1. Opens app / website
  2. Selects service type
  3. Picks date / time / duration
  4. Sees available providers
  5. Books provider (instant or scheduled)
  6. Gets provider ETA + GPS tracking
  7. Provider arrives → body cam activates
  8. Watches live feed (optional)
  9. Session ends → auto-charged
  10. Rates & reviews provider

Provider (Employee App)

  1. Goes "online" (available)
  2. Sets service area (5-25 mi radius)
  3. Receives job notification
  4. Sees job details + pay estimate
  5. Accepts or declines (60s timer)
  6. Navigates to family location
  7. Starts session → body cam ON
  8. Completes care tasks
  9. Session ends → payment deposited
  10. Rates family → earns tips

Tipping System

After each session ends, the family receives a tip prompt screen with options: 15%, 20%, 25%, or Custom. The default is pre-selected at 20%. 100% of tips go directly to the provider — KinCare takes $0 from tips. Tipping is optional but prominently encouraged.
